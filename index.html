Five ways
to train

Pick one or mix them. Every session can be shaped around your goal, whether that is strength, fat loss or just getting started.

  • Weight training

    Weights and equipment to build strength and muscle, at a level that suits you.

  • Personal training

    One-on-one coaching built around your goal, your schedule and where you are starting from.

  • Nutritional training

    Practical eating guidance that goes with your training, for fat loss or for building muscle.

  • Cycling

    Low-impact cardio for stamina and fat loss that is easy on the joints.

  • Treadmill

    Walk, jog or run. Warm up, build endurance or burn calories, whatever the weather outside.
